# BabiesPicks Design Context

## Product
AI-powered Saudi baby & maternity product review platform. Bilingual (Arabic default, English). RTL-first.

## Users
- **Primary:** Saudi moms (first-time and experienced) researching baby products
- **Emotional state:** Anxious about making the right choice, seeking trust and credibility
- **Goal:** Confident purchase decisions backed by AI-verified reviews

## Brand Personality
**Trustworthy · Warm · Decisive**

- Builds confidence through clear verdicts (يستاهل / لا يستاهل)
- Feels like advice from a knowledgeable friend, not a cold review site
- Drives action — every page should push toward a purchase decision

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Style:** Modern, warm, organic — NOT sharp/angular or dated
- **Mood:** Soft, approachable, credible
- **Light mode only** (current palette is approved)
- Current colors and design direction are approved — enhance, don't redesign

## Design Tokens (from globals.css @theme)
- **Primary:** sage (#6B8E7F), sage-deep (#2D4A2D), sage-mid (#5C8A5C)
- **Backgrounds:** cream (#FAF8F5), linen (#F4F1EC)
- **Text:** charcoal (#2D3A35), stone (#5F6660)
- **Borders:** beige (#E8E2D8), warm-beige (#E8D5C4)
- **Accents:** lavender (#F0EAF8), terracotta (#B85542)
- **Verdict:** good (green), conditional (amber), wait (lavender), bad (red)
- **Radii:** card 12px, button 8px, badge 12px, pill 9999px
- **Fonts:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic) + Inter (Latin)
- **Icons:** Tabler Icons (webfont CDN)

## Anti-references
- Sharp/angular corporate designs
- Dated/old-fashioned layouts
- Clinical/cold medical aesthetics
- Generic AI-generated look

## Accessibility
- Target: **WCAG AA**
- Support RTL and LTR layouts
- Respect reduced motion preferences
- Ensure sufficient color contrast on all verdict badges

## Tech Stack
- Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4 (v4 — config in globals.css @theme, no tailwind.config.js)
- Path alias: `@/*` → `./src/*`
- next-intl for i18n (ar/en, localePrefix: 'always')
- Components in `src/shared/components/`
